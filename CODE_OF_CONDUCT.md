# Code of conduct

Be decent. Specifically:

**Assume competence.** The person who filed the confusing issue is usually
debugging something real under time pressure. So is the person whose patch you are
reviewing.

**Argue about the work, not the person.** "This merges two different defects, here
is the case" is useful. "This is obviously wrong" is not, even when it is.

**A report that buildclamp broke someone's build is urgent.** They cannot ship until it is resolved, and they were doing the right thing by trying the tool. Answer the technical question and skip the lecture.
sometimes wrong, occasionally both. Answer the technical question and skip the
lecture. If a report is out of scope, say so in a sentence and move on.

**Do not disclose what is not yours to disclose.** Reports handled in this
project's own security process, or shared with us in confidence, stay that way
until the reporter says otherwise.

Not acceptable: harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate
intimidation, or publishing someone's private information. This applies in issues,
pull requests, commits, and anywhere else the project's name is on the door.

## Enforcement

Report to **the maintainer**. Reports go to a human who will not discuss
them beyond what is needed to act, and will tell you what happened.

Responses run from a private word to a permanent ban, depending. We will not
pretend there is a tariff.

If the person you need to report is the maintainer, say so in the mail; it will
still be read, and you are entitled to a reply that is not a defence.
